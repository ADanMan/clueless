# 2026-10-01 — v1.1 (five lenses, tagged figures, ultra leak fix) vs v1.0

Same three prompts, three runs each, same grader and checklists. Only the skill changed; the baseline set from [2026-10-01-n3-baseline-vs-clueless.md](2026-10-01-n3-baseline-vs-clueless.md) is reused. Answers in [../runs-v1.1/](../runs-v1.1/), grades in [../scores-v1.1.json](../scores-v1.1.json).

## What changed in the skill

1. Step 1 ("what would an expert have asked") replaced by five concrete lenses: money, paperwork, the other human's mistake, twelve months out, personal data.
2. Unverified figures must carry an inline tag: `30% (estimate, confirm with your CPA)`.
3. The "You asked for X; what you actually want is Y" opening moved to the `ultra` level only, with an explicit line that `full` starts at step 1.

No change in the total. What moved:

- Figures tagged as estimates: 2/3 → 3/3 (taxes). The structural rule worked.
- W-9 / 1099 paperwork: 0/3 → 2/3. The paperwork lens worked.
- Ultra reframing at `full`: 2 of 9 answers → 0 of 9. The leak fix worked.
- Login rate limiting: 3/3 → 1/3 (signup). Lost. Plausibly noise at n=3, plausibly the longer lens list crowded out the security pass. Unknown.
- Still 0/3: first-year safe-harbor rule, unpaid invoices / contract + deposit. The money lens did not surface them. These look like genuine knowledge gaps at this model size rather than shape problems.
- Password reset: still BURIED in all three. The model mentions it every time, but in "Careful" or "Only you can decide", never as a built or mandatory step.

## Decision

Kept v1.1. Same score, three structural properties gained (tagged figures, paperwork surfaced, no ultra leak), one possible regression (rate limit) that n=3 cannot resolve. Next candidate change: make "the other human's mistake" lens require a mitigation *in the deliverable*, not a mention, to move password reset from BURIED to PASS.

# Setting Up Automatic Backups for Your MacBook

Good news: macOS has a built-in backup tool called **Time Machine** that, once set up, runs automatically forever. You won't need to think about it again. I'll also set up a cloud backup so you're protected even if something happens to your house/laptop at the same time (fire, theft, etc.) — that's the "3-2-1 rule" professionals use, but you don't need to know that, just follow the steps.

## Part 1: Time Machine (local backup) — protects against accidental deletion, corrupted files, etc.

1. **Buy an external hard drive.** Get one at least double the size of your MacBook's storage (e.g., if your Mac has 512GB, get a 1TB+ drive). A simple USB-C external SSD from a store like Best Buy or Amazon works fine — no need to research brands extensively, any reputable one (Samsung, SanDisk, WD) is fine.
2. **Plug the drive into your MacBook.**
3. A popup will appear asking "Do you want to use this disk to back up with Time Machine?" → Click **Use as Backup Disk**.
4. It may ask if you want to **encrypt the backup** — click yes, and set a password you'll remember (this protects your data if the drive is ever lost or stolen).
5. Click **Use Disk**. That's it — it will now back up automatically every hour in the background, and keep daily backups for a month and weekly backups older than that.
6. Leave the drive plugged in as much as possible (e.g., at your desk). It only needs to be connected to back up — no action needed from you after this.

## Part 2: Cloud backup — protects your freelance folder (contracts, invoices, designs) even if your laptop and external drive are both lost

1. Open **System Settings** → click your name at the top (**Apple ID**) → **iCloud**.
2. Turn on **iCloud Drive**.
3. Turn on **Desktop & Documents Folders** sync — this automatically uploads anything in your Desktop and Documents folders to the cloud and keeps it updated in real time.
4. **Move your freelance folder into your Documents folder** (drag it there in Finder) so it's covered by this sync.
5. You get 5GB free with Apple; if your photos/files are large, go to the same iCloud settings screen and click **Upgrade** to get 200GB (~$2.99/month) — worth it for peace of mind.

## Part 3: Photos specifically

1. Open **System Settings** → **Apple ID** → **iCloud** → **Photos**.
2. Turn on **iCloud Photos**. This automatically uploads every photo and keeps them backed up and accessible from your phone too.

## That's it — what happens now, automatically, forever:

- **Every hour:** Time Machine backs up your whole Mac to the external drive.
- **Continuously:** Your Documents (including freelance work) and Photos sync to Apple's cloud.
- **You do nothing else.** No reminders, no manual steps, no maintenance.

## The only two things to remember:
- Keep the external drive plugged in when you're home/at your desk.
- If you ever get a new Mac, just log into iCloud and plug in the Time Machine drive — everything restores itself.

If anything ever goes wrong (accidentally delete a file, laptop breaks), just tell whoever's helping you "I have Time Machine and iCloud backups" and they'll know exactly what to do.

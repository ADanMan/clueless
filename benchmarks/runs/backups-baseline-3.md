## Your Backup Plan (set once, forget about it)

You need two things running: one for your whole Mac (so if it dies you get everything back), and one specifically for your freelance folder (so a client's contract is never just "gone"). Here's the simplest setup that covers both.

### Step 1: Turn on Time Machine (backs up literally everything, automatically)

1. Buy an external hard drive — any USB drive at least 2x the size of your MacBook's storage (e.g., if you have a 512GB Mac, get a 1TB+ drive). A basic one costs $50-70 on Amazon.
2. Plug it into your MacBook.
3. A popup will ask "Do you want to use this disk to back up with Time Machine?" → Click **Encrypt Backup Disk** (it'll ask you to set a password — write it down somewhere safe), then click **Use as Backup Disk**.
4. That's it. It now backs up automatically every hour in the background, forever, as long as you plug the drive in periodically (leaving it connected most of the time is fine).

This protects you if your laptop is lost, stolen, dies, or gets a virus.

### Step 2: Turn on iCloud Photos (backs up your photos off-site automatically)

1. Click the Apple menu → **System Settings** → click your name at the top → **iCloud**.
2. Turn on **Photos**.
3. If prompted about storage, upgrade your iCloud plan — 200GB is $2.99/month, 2TB is $9.99/month. For most people with a lot of photos, get 200GB to start; you'll get a warning if you need more.

Now your photos live in the cloud too, not just on the external drive sitting in your house. This matters because Time Machine won't save you if your apartment floods or burns down with both laptop and drive inside.

### Step 3: Back up your freelance folder to the cloud separately (contracts, invoices, designs)

This is the one you really can't afford to lose, and it deserves its own safety net beyond Time Machine.

1. Find your freelance folder in Finder.
2. Drag it into your iCloud Drive (or Dropbox/Google Drive if you already use one of those for client sharing — doesn't matter which, just pick one).
   - Easiest: Finder sidebar → **iCloud Drive** → drag your folder in there.
3. Make sure "Desktop & Documents Folders" sync is on too, as a backstop: System Settings → iCloud → iCloud Drive → toggle on **Desktop & Documents Folders**.

Now that folder is synced to the cloud continuously, not just backed up once an hour — so even a change five minutes ago is already safe elsewhere.

### That's the whole setup. Here's why this is enough:

- **Laptop stolen/dies** → restore everything from Time Machine drive onto a new Mac.
- **Time Machine drive also lost/damaged** → photos are in iCloud, freelance files are in iCloud Drive.
- **You accidentally delete a client file** → find it in iCloud Drive's recently deleted, or restore from Time Machine (which keeps hourly snapshots going back weeks/months).

### One thing to actually do periodically (takes 10 seconds)

Every couple of weeks, glance at the Time Machine icon in your menu bar (or plug the drive in if you've been leaving it unplugged) to make sure it's still backing up. If you ever see a red exclamation mark, just plug the drive in and let it catch up.

That's genuinely it — no further learning, no maintenance, no manual "backing up" required going forward.
